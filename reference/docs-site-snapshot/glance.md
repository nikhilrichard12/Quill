# Glance: your day at a glance
A collapsible panel on the home page that surfaces your schedule, routines, recent meetings, and more so you land with context already in view.

## What Glance does

Glance is a panel on the right side of the home (new chat) page that gives you a quick snapshot of your day before you even type a prompt. Instead of opening your calendar, checking your routines, and hunting for yesterday’s meeting notes separately, Glance pulls them together in one place.

Toggle the panel with Cmd+G (Ctrl+G on Windows) or click the Glance pill.

## Widgets

Glance is made up of widgets, each surfacing a different part of your day:

- Schedule - upcoming events and meetings from your connected calendar

- Routines - today’s routines and their status

- Yesterday’s meetings - recent meeting summaries so you can pick up where you left off

- Continue - jump back into your most recent chat

- Connect more - shortcuts to connect additional integrations

Widgets with no data yet show a placeholder card explaining what will appear there once you connect a calendar, create a routine, and so on.

## Customizing Glance

Click the cog icon in the panel header to show, hide, or reorder widgets. Your layout and whether the panel is open or closed are remembered across sessions.

On narrow windows, the panel hides automatically so the chat composer keeps full width.

Note

Glance is just getting started. More widgets are on the way, including widgets powered by your connected integrations and proactive surfacing of things that need your attention. Use Give Feedback in the app or the Discord community to tell us what widgets you’d find useful.
