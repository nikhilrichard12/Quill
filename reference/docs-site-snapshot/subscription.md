# Subscription
Upgrade, change, or cancel your Littlebird subscription from the account controls, with no hunting through settings.

## Upgrade your plan

Open your profile menu, then select Settings → Subscription to choose an available paid plan.

If your trial has expired, you can also start the upgrade from the lower-left corner of Littlebird.

Choose a plan on the Subscription page. Compare the current Littlebird plans.

## Cancel your Littlebird subscription

After you cancel, your access continues until the end of the current billing period.

### Cancel through the app

- Open Littlebird, select your profile icon in the bottom-left corner, and go to Settings.

- Select Subscription from the left menu.

- Select Manage Subscription to open the billing portal in your browser.

- In the portal, select Cancel Subscription.

- Confirm the cancellation. You’ll receive an email from us confirming it went through.

Note: Your subscription will not auto-renew after cancellation, and you won’t be charged going forward.

### If the option is missing

If the subscription page is blank or the cancel option is missing, email us:

Email: support@littlebird.ai

Include the email address associated with your Littlebird account.

### Frequently asked questions

Can I reactivate later?

Yes. You can resubscribe from the Subscription page in Settings.

I’m on a free trial - do I need to cancel?

If you signed up for a trial and don’t want to be charged, yes - please cancel before your trial ends to avoid being billed. The steps above are the same.

Still need help? Email support@littlebird.ai.
