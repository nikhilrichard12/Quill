# Outlook
Bring Outlook mail and calendar into Littlebird so Chat can help without waiting for Outlook to be open, with Hummingbird on Mac too.

The Outlook integration lets Littlebird work with your calendar and email even when Outlook is not open. You can currently connect one Outlook account.

Once connected, Littlebird can read your schedule, create events, search your inbox, draft replies, and more from Chat. On macOS, you can also use Hummingbird Quick Access (not available on Windows).

## How to connect

- Open Settings → Integrations.

- Find Outlook, then select Connect.

- Sign in with your Microsoft account and approve access.

- Confirm that Outlook appears as active in your settings.

## What you can do

### Calendar

With your Outlook calendar connected, Littlebird can:

- View your schedule and upcoming events

- Create, edit, and delete calendar events

- Cancel events and notify attendees

- Find available meeting times

- Check coworker availability

Try these prompts:

Prompt

What does my schedule look like for the rest of this week? Flag any conflicts or back-to-back blocks.

Prompt

Schedule a 30-minute meeting with [name] sometime Thursday afternoon. Find a time that works for both of us.

### Reading email

Littlebird can search and read your Outlook email directly:

-

Search for emails by keyword, sender, or date across all folders or within a specific one

-

Read full message content

-

Move emails across folders

-

View attachment info (file names, sizes, and types)

- Cannot download and view underlying attachment content

Try these prompts:

Prompt

Find the most recent email from [name] about the project timeline.

Prompt

Show me any unread emails in my inbox from the last 24 hours. Organize them by urgency.

### Drafting and organizing email

Littlebird can also help you compose and manage messages:

-

Draft new emails, replies, and forwards

- These will then exist in your Drafts folder

-

Move messages between folders

-

Mark messages as read or unread

-

Flag and categorize messages

Try these prompts:

Prompt

Draft a reply to [name]’s email about the contract review. Keep the tone professional and confirm the Friday deadline.

Prompt

Move all emails from [sender] to my “Reviewed” folder.

Note

Tip: Try quickly scheduling a calendar event or drafting an email with Hummingbird Quick Access (macOS only; use Chat on Windows)

## What’s not currently supported

To keep the integration secure and focused, a few actions are not available:

- Sending emails. Littlebird can draft emails for you, but sending happens on your end. This keeps you in full control of what goes out

- Downloading or adding attachments. Littlebird can see attachment info but can’t open, download, or attach files

- Inbox rules. Creating or modifying mail flow rules is not supported

- Deleting emails. Littlebird can move and organize messages but can’t delete them

## Troubleshooting

The integration isn’t connecting. Check whether your organization requires admin approval for third-party app access. If you’re on a personal Microsoft account, try signing out and reconnecting from Settings → Integrations.

Littlebird can’t find my calendar events. Make sure the events are on the primary calendar associated with your connected account. Shared or delegated calendars may not sync automatically.

I connected Outlook but Littlebird is still only reading my screen. After connecting, try asking a direct question about your calendar or email (e.g., “What meetings do I have tomorrow?”). The integration activates when Littlebird needs to pull data beyond what’s visible on your screen.
