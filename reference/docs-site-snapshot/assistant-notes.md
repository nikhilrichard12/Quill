# Assistant Notes
Give Littlebird persistent memory and standing rules so it starts every conversation already knowing your preferences.

## User-editable persistent memory

Littlebird remembers things across your conversations in several ways, but two of them can be directly edited by you:

### Custom Instructions

Text you write in Settings → Chat → Custom Instructions. These are standing rules that apply to every conversation. Only you can edit them – they cannot be modified from chat.

Examples:

- “Always respond in Spanish”

- “Keep responses under 3 paragraphs”

- “My team uses Jira for project tracking”

### Assistant Notes

Editable in Settings → Chat → Assistant Notes, or directly from chat. Littlebird can add, update, or remove items when you ask it to.

Just say things like:

- “Remember that I have two kids, two cats, and one husband”

- “From now on, call me Chief”

- “I prefer bullet points over long paragraphs”

- “Remember to cross check my todos with this Notion board”

## What to save

Assistant Notes are best for important, durable facts you want Littlebird to always know – your preferences, key relationships, ongoing projects, and standing context. You don’t need to save everything you want in memory: Littlebird can already search your past conversations, screen context, and connected integrations directly. Save the things that should always be applied proactively, not recalled on demand.

## What sticks and what doesn’t

Assistant Notes stay intact during periods of inactivity. If you go on vacation, everything Littlebird has learned will be waiting when you get back.
