# Set up
Give Littlebird a useful picture of your work by checking Context Awareness and connecting the tools you rely on.

## Your first couple of days with Littlebird

### Start with the work that matters

Littlebird starts building context as soon as you install it. To give it a useful first picture of your work, spend a little time in the apps you use most — your email, notes, project tools, and calendar are good places to begin.

You can check that Context Awareness is active in two places:

-

In the Littlebird app, look in the bottom-left corner.

- A green light means Context Awareness is on.

-

In the menu bar at the top of your Mac screen.

-

If Context Awareness is paused, you’ll see a pause icon beside the bird.

### Connect your calendar and email

Littlebird may have asked you to connect your email and calendar during setup. If you skipped that step, you can connect them now.

#### How to connect

You only need to connect each account once.

-

Select your name in the bottom-left corner, then go to Settings → Integrations.

-

Find Google Calendar and turn it on.

-

You will be redirected to a Google authentication screen. Choose the Google account you want to connect.

-

Review the permissions, then select Allow.

-

When you return to Littlebird, the integration should show as Active.

Note

Littlebird offers hundreds of optional integrations. Open Settings → Integrations for the current list, including Gmail, Google Calendar, Microsoft Outlook, Apple Calendar, and Apple Reminders.
