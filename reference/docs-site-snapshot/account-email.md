# Email address
Move your Littlebird account to a new email address and verify the change without losing your existing password.

## How to change your account email address

You can move your Littlebird account to a new email address from settings.

- Open settings: Select your profile in the lower-left corner, then select Settings.

- Open the General tab: Find your current email address.

- Start the change: Select the pencil icon beside the address.

- Enter the new address: Littlebird sends a one-time password to the new address and a notification to the old one.

- Confirm the address: Enter the one-time password. Littlebird signs you out after the change.

- Sign in again: Use the new email address and your existing password.
