# First meeting
Let Littlebird handle the transcript and summary while you stay present for your first recorded meeting.

## Let Littlebird take the notes

Littlebird’s Meeting Notes transcribes and summarizes your meetings, leaving you free to listen and take part.

## Two ways to transcribe

-

From a reminder: A few minutes before a calendar event, Littlebird sends a notification. Select Join Meeting to begin transcribing.

-

From Meeting Notes: Open Meeting Notes, select + New Note, and start transcribing.

When the meeting ends, Littlebird gives you a transcript and summary to review.
